Visit

The fresco room is reserved for six to ten guests.

Hidden beyond the main salon, the private dining room hosts family celebrations, collector dinners, and quiet business meals.

Private Rooms

A dedicated sommelier and a menu printed overnight.

The fresco room includes a separate arrival, hand-written menu cards, and a dedicated service team. Lunch is available for private reservations only.

Hours

Dinner Tuesday to Saturday. Doors open at 19:00 and the final arrival is 21:15.

Dress

Jackets are appreciated in the main salon. Sportswear is not suitable for dinner.

Arrival

Casa Aurelia is a ten-minute walk from Santa Maria Novella and has valet on request.
