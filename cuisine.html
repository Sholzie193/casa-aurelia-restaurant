Cuisine

Il Salone

The room is deliberately small, the service is low-voiced, and the food carries the memory of Tuscany without becoming nostalgic.

Brigata

Service that moves quietly around the evening.

Linen is pressed in-house, bread is served from a silver guéridon, and the dining room is kept deliberately small so the tempo can follow each table rather than the clock.

Produce

From market, coast, and estate garden.

The kitchen buys fish before dawn in Livorno, works with two Chianina farms in Valdarno, and keeps a small garden above Settignano for herbs, bitter leaves, and summer tomatoes.

Technique

Italian clarity, French discipline.

Stocks are clarified, pasta is rolled to order, and sauces are reduced until they feel inevitable. The plate is never busier than the idea behind it.
