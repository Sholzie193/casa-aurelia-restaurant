Formats

Dinner is served as a progression.

The full menu is eight courses. A six-course vegetarian menu is available with advance notice. Both can be paired from the cellar or with a non-alcoholic progression built in the kitchen.

Guest notes

Dietary details are handled before arrival.

The brigade can accommodate most restrictions with 72 hours of notice. The menu is not offered à la carte.
